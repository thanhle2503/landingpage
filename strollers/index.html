Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

When can a baby sit in a stroller seat?

Most seats are for babies who can hold their head up, around 6 months. Before that, use a flat seat or bassinet.

Single or double stroller?

If a second baby is coming within two years, a stroller that converts to a double can save buying twice.
