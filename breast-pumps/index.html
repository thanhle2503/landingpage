MamiPicks notes: which pump fits which mum

How we would choose, based on what each brand publishes.

Back at work or always on the go: a wearable pump fits inside your bra, so you can pump hands-free.

Pumping often at home: a stronger electric pump empties faster.

Check the flange size: the right fit matters more than the brand; most pumps include two sizes.

Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

Wearable or regular electric pump?

Wearable pumps are quiet and hands-free; regular electric pumps are usually stronger. Many mums use both.

Can I use insurance for a breast pump?

Many US insurance plans cover one pump. Check your plan before buying.
