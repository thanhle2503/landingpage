                  Questions, answered

                  Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

                  When should I test for ovulation?

                  Start a few days before your expected ovulation and test at the same time each day.

                  How early can a pregnancy test work?

                  Many tests can show a result around the day of your missed period. Testing later is more reliable.
