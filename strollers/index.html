                              Questions, answered

                              Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

                              When can a baby sit in a stroller seat?

                              Most seats are for babies who can hold their head up, around 6 months. Before that, use a flat seat, bassinet or car seat adapter.

                              Do side-by-side doubles fit through doors?

                              Valco lists the Slim Twin as narrow; check its width against your doors.
