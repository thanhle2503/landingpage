# MamiPicks

> MamiPicks (https://mom.goldenpasss.com/) is an independent comparison site for parents in the US. It sorts baby products by age (newborn to 5 years) and mum products by stage (trying to conceive, pregnancy, postpartum, breastfeeding and pumping, back to work), and compares them by brand, price and who each one suits.

Quick facts:
- Brands covered: Valco Baby, Momcozy, Frida, Evereden, MomMed, Kindred Bravely, FullWell, Amaana, Momomi.
- Baby picks by age: 0–6 months, 6–12 months, 1–2 years, 2–3 years, 3–5 years, each with tips and safety notes.
- Mum advisor: answer 3–4 questions for one of six situations (trying for a baby, pregnant, just had a baby, breastfeeding or pumping, going back to work, taking care of me) and get six picks.
- Every product has a short MamiPicks note: what it is, who it is good for, when to skip it, pros and cons.
- Prices come from each brand's store or Amazon listing on the date shown on each page; the price at checkout applies.
- MamiPicks earns affiliate commissions (including as an Amazon Associate) at no extra cost to buyers. Not medical advice.
