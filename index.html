MamiPicks notes

What helps at this age
  • Start with the basics: swaddles, a gentle wash and a few muslins. Big toys can wait.
  • A carrier gives you two free hands in the first months.
Safety first
  • Put baby to sleep on their back on a flat, empty surface. No pillows, bumpers or loose blankets.
  • Stop swaddling as soon as baby shows signs of rolling.
