                        How we would choose, based on what each brand publishes.

                        Questions, answered

                        Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

                        What do I need for postpartum recovery?

                        Most mums use maxi pads, cooling pad liners and a peri bottle in the first weeks. A kit bundles them.

                        Are stretch-mark products safe in pregnancy?

                        Evereden labels these as pregnancy and postpartum safe. Ask your doctor if unsure.
