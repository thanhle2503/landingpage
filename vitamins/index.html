Questions, answered

When should I start a prenatal vitamin?

Doctors usually suggest starting a folate-containing prenatal at least a month before trying to conceive.

Can I keep taking it while breastfeeding?

Many mums do, or switch to a postnatal formula. Ask your doctor.
